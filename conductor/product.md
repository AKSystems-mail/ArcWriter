# Product Definition: ArcWriter

## Initial Concept
ArcWriter is an AI-powered SaaS platform designed to assist screenwriters throughout the film development lifecycle. It aims to guide writers from brainstorming to generating media assets and ultimately pitching their movies.

## Target Audience
- **Professional Screenwriters:** Seeking structural narrative rigor and high-fidelity continuity management.
- **Aspiring Filmmakers & "Pantsers":** Individuals needing guidance in the brainstorming and world-building phase (Explorer mode).
- **Script Doctors & Producers:** Professionals interested in stress-testing plot holes and narrative logic using mathematical models (Logic Engine).

## Core Value Proposition
ArcWriter transforms the solitary and often disorganized writing process into a guided, data-driven pipeline. By combining a "Universe Bible" for continuity, a "Game Theory Logic Engine" for plot integrity, and automated "Pitch Deck Generation," it helps writers not just write, but effectively sell their stories.

## Key Features (Launch Focus)
- **Dual-Mode Onboarding:** catering to both "Pantsers" (Explorer) and "Plotters" (Architect).
- **Universe Architecture:** A RAG-powered project bible to maintain consistency across scripts.
- **Game Theory Logic Engine:** Mathematical stress-testing to identify and resolve plot holes.
- **Pitch Deck Automation:** Automatically assembling script data, characters, and structural beats into a professional, ready-to-present pitch package.

## Success Metrics
- **Narrative Consistency:** High percentage of plot holes identified and resolved by users via the Logic Engine.
- **Platform Loyalty (User Retention):** Percentage of users who complete at least two separate scripts or "Universes" within the platform.
